TaskDeck

TaskDeck is a native macOS productivity application designed to organize, manage, and synchronize your Google Tasks and Google Calendar events into a unified desktop workspace.

App Purpose & Functionality

TaskDeck helps users manage daily task lists and schedule time blocks on a visual board. By connecting to Google APIs, TaskDeck enables users to view, create, edit, and complete tasks from Google Tasks, as well as view calendar events from Google Calendar directly from their Mac.

Data Usage & Privacy

TaskDeck processes all task and calendar data locally on your device. Authentication tokens are securely stored in the native macOS Keychain. Your Google user data is never stored on third-party servers, tracked, or sold.
