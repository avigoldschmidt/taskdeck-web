Privacy Policy for TaskDeck

Last updated: August 2026

TaskDeck operates as a standalone, native desktop application for macOS. We prioritize your privacy and data security by ensuring your information stays on your device.

1. Data Collection & API Usage

TaskDeck requests access to the Google Tasks API and Google Calendar API. This access is used strictly to display, manage, and synchronize your tasks and calendar events directly within the macOS interface.

2. Local Data Storage

Your data never touches our servers. All Google authentication tokens, tasks, and calendar entries are processed and stored completely locally on your machine. TaskDeck utilizes the native macOS Keychain to securely store your OAuth credentials and authorization state.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not collect, sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to any external servers, analytics providers, or third parties. Your data moves directly and securely between your local TaskDeck application and Google's official API servers.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact the developer at: support@agoldschmidt.com
